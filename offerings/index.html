Working together

A steady rhythm.
Something real.

Some sessions we build. Some we think through what you’re trying to bring into the world. Both matter.

I start with your practice and the tools around it. These are the shapes of ongoing support; I’ll work with you to decide what fits.

Monthly session shapes · draft offer

01

Two sessions a month

2 × 90 minutes / month

A regular thinking partner for direction, decisions, and practical next steps. A session can be split into 60 + 30 minutes when that helps.

A biweekly rhythm for getting clearer about what to do next.

02

Four sessions a month

4 × 90 minutes / month

More room to make things together: site pages, intake forms, course setup, or a small tool. Building happens live, with and for you.

For learning by doing, with someone alongside you.

03

Sessions + off-call building

4 × 90 minutes / month + about 2½ hours of building / week

Our conversations guide the work, and I carry an agreed part of the build between sessions. The off-call time is bounded; I’m not offering an unlimited development team.

For a project that needs sustained attention beyond our calls.

Pricing on request. I’ll agree on scope and terms with you before we begin.

In and between sessions

Keep the thread.
Keep your agency.

We can use our time to clarify an offering, choose tools, work through a problem, or build. I want you to understand the result, not depend on me to make every change.

I’m developing a supporting layer of session transcripts, summaries, and suggested next steps. It isn’t a guaranteed part of this draft offer yet. Before using it, I’ll need to agree with you on recording, AI use, access, and deletion.

I work on your tools and creative direction. I don’t offer clinical treatment or mental-health services.

A smaller starting point

One thing to untangle.

A focused session or a small website project may be enough. I’ll help define what’s included, what you’ll be able to maintain, and what needs a separate project.

For organizations

A separate conversation.

If you’re part of a small, mission-oriented team, I can discuss a defined website or systems need. Organizational work is separately scoped, not another practitioner tier.
