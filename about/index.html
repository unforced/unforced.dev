Who I am

I’m Aaron.
I listen. I build.

The technical and the human aren’t separate parts of my work. Conversation helps me understand what’s worth making.

A little background

Software, people,
and living systems.

I live in Boulder, Colorado. My background includes computer science at Case Western, developer relations at Google, and a master’s in Ecopsychology from Naropa. I’ve also studied Creative Technology & Design at CU Boulder’s ATLAS Institute.

Ecopsychology and embodied practices, including Tai Ji, inform how I pay attention. Software gives me a way to turn that attention into something useful.

I’ve developed Learn Vibe Build to help people create with AI, and I build Parachute around the question of how our tools can support more connected thinking.

Why Unforced

My dad built houses.
I build with words.

My dad, Joe, was a builder and a Tai Ji teacher. He gave me the word Unforced, and a way of approaching creation: listen to what’s there, find a workable shape, and participate in how it grows.

For me, that doesn’t mean being passive. In a working relationship it means listening carefully, naming the next step, and agreeing on what I’ll do.

I’m interested in helping people feel more able to make and care for their own things. Sometimes that means paid support. Sometimes it means pointing you toward a way to do it yourself.
