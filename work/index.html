Work / public examples

From a conversation
into something usable.

These are projects from my own building and learning practice, not client testimonials. They show different ways I turn a question into a working tool.

01 / Open-source tool

NatalEngine

Read the published story ↗

The question

A conversation about chart-based collaboration brought an old idea back into focus. Could I make the underlying calculations accessible without bundling them into a closed app?

What I made

I built a JavaScript library for astrology, Human Design, and Gene Keys chart calculations, with an AI integration and a simple calculator interface.

What it makes possible

Other builders can use the structured chart data in their own tools. The library separates calculation from interpretation; I don’t present it as scientific evidence or a care service.

An example of building around a real conversation. Not a promise of a weekend turnaround for your project.

02 / My own ongoing project

Parachute

Read about the development process ↗

The question

My notes and thinking were scattered across tools. I wanted a connected place to work with them.

What I made

I developed a knowledge-and-memory layer that stores notes, tags, and links, with interfaces for people and AI tools to work with the same material.

What I learned

Building more wasn’t always the answer. Returning to conversations helped me see where I was over-engineering and bring the work back to a simpler foundation.

Ongoing work, not a claim that a client-facing session system is ready.

03 / Learning by making

Learn Vibe Build

Read the invitation ↗

The question

How can people with ideas, but little programming experience, begin to build with AI?

What I made

I developed a curriculum and learning platform around making real projects together, rather than just following tutorials.

What it makes possible

A place to practice turning an idea into something you can try, understand, and change. That learn-by-doing approach also informs my one-to-one work.
